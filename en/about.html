ABOUT GENIVA

A connected view
of genomic workflows.

Geniva Bilişim Sağlık A.Ş. develops software, hardware and training solutions for the effective use and analysis of genomic data.

We bring together the Gennext platform, on-site computing infrastructure, laboratory services and medical genomics training. Our multi-omics approach considers genomic, transcriptomic, epigenetic, metabolomic and lipidomic data in a shared biological context.

We focus on understanding the needs of healthcare professionals and researchers, developing technical solutions that support scientific evaluation.
