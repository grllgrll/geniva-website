01 / GENNEXT

Sequencing data.
Findings you can assess.

We analyse short-read and long-read sequencing data, from quality control and variant assessment to reporting. Gennext Clinical supports somatic and germline workflows; Gennext Longevity brings genetic and epigenetic findings into professional wellness services.

SHORT-READ

Short-read analysis

Processing of panel, exome and whole-genome sequencing data, with variant filtering, interpretation and reporting.

LONG-READ

Long-read analysis

Processing and assessment of long-read sequencing data. Analysis scope is defined by the data type and research question.

Somatic analysis

Oncology and molecular pathology

Assessment of variants, fusions and biomarkers from tumour sequencing data, with reports supporting specialist review.

Germline analysis

Inherited conditions and reproductive health

Variant assessment in the clinical context of rare disease, carrier screening and pharmacogenetic studies.

Longevity

Genetic and epigenetic assessment

Genetic predisposition profiles across health, nutrition, sport and skin, alongside epigenetic age assessments. Reports support personalised professional guidance.

Longevity reports support personalised health guidance; they are not diagnostic tests.

Your analysis infrastructure.
At your institution.
