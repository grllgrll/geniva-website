A broader biological context

Our multi-omics approach considers genomic, transcriptomic, epigenetic, metabolomic and lipidomic data together. Genetic predisposition and epigenetic assessments support personalised health and wellness services.

Longevity reports support personalised health guidance; they are not diagnostic tests.
